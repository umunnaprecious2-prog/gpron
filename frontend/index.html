Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before placing your first order.

Create a free account, log in to your dashboard, list your items, choose Normal or Express service, and submit. You will receive a unique GPRON tracking ID immediately.
Normal service has a 3 to 5 day turnaround starting at ₦500 base. Express service guarantees a 24-hour turnaround starting at ₦900 base with priority handling and premium ironing.
Every order gets a unique tracking ID in the format GPRON-2026-XXXX. Enter it on our Track page or view progress from your dashboard. You can see exactly where your clothes are at every stage.
We handle all common fabrics including cotton, linen, wool, silk, and synthetic blends. Our specialists also handle delicate items such as wedding dresses, traditional Ankara and Aso-Oke, suits, and bedding.
